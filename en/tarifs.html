Frequently asked questions

Pricing and getting started

How much does Lago cost?

+

Nothing: Lago is free, with the POS, customer scheduling, checks and gift cards, and unlimited users. You only pay for the options you choose, from €3.99 excl. VAT per month, and a 2% commission on online payments on top of Stripe fees.

Is Lago really free?

+

Yes. There is no subscription, no credit card asked and no trial period: the core software stays free. Lago is funded by the options and the commission on online payments.

How do I download Lago?

+

Lago installs in one click from your browser, on a tablet, computer or phone, without going through an app store. Go to the “Download for free” page.

What commission applies to online payments?

+

Lago takes 2% of each online payment (booking), on top of Stripe’s fees. There is no subscription or monthly minimum, and no Lago commission on in-store POS sales.

Do I need to buy hardware?

+

No. Lago runs on the tablet, computer or phone you already have, with your usual payment terminal.

Is there a commitment?

+

No. Options are monthly with no commitment: you can stop them at the end of any month, and your data remains exportable.

How do I buy an option?

+

In the app, open the “App Store” menu, pick the option and pay by card with Stripe: it switches on automatically. You can stop it at any time from the subscription page.
