What changes with Lago

Departures every 15 minutes, with no double bookings

At the till or online, every sale books its departure in the same schedule. Lago shows the remaining places per quarter hour: the team sees immediately if a departure is full.

  • Capacity of each departure respected
  • Front-desk and website bookings in one place
  • Wristband colour by time

Frequently asked questions

Go-karting: your questions

Does Lago handle lap timing?

+

No. Lago handles the POS, bookings, waivers, team and checks. Lap timing stays with your usual system.

How are waivers signed?

+

On a tablet at reception, directly in Lago. A waiver stays valid for a year and can be found in an instant. Have your text reviewed by your insurer: our guide to liability waivers (in French) explains what they allow, and their limits.

Can I sell packs of several sessions?

+

Yes, with session passes: the customer buys a pack, and each session is counted down when they come.
