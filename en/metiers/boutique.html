Frequently asked questions

Retail shops: your questions

Do I need to buy specific hardware?

+

No. Lago runs in the browser and installs like an app on the tablet, computer or phone you already have.

Is Lago compliant for selling to consumers in France?

+

Lago is designed to meet the French requirements for inalterability, security, retention and archiving, and provides the publisher’s certificate based on the official template.

Can I sell gift cards?

+

Yes. Gift cards are created and redeemed directly at the till.
