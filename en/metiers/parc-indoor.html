Frequently asked questions

Indoor play centres: your questions

Can I sell the child’s and the accompanying adult’s entry on the same ticket?

+

Yes. Each entry is a POS item, and you choose which ones count towards capacity.

How do birthday parties work?

+

You create an event with its room, times and number of guests. It appears in the customer schedule, you can prepare a quote and ask for an online deposit.

Does Lago replace the inspection log?

+

Lago records your checks with the date, time, person and any issues, as PDFs. Check with your manufacturer, insurer or inspection body what content and frequency are expected for your equipment.
