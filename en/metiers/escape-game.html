What changes with Lago

Online bookings, with a deposit if you wish

You publish your available slots on your website. The customer picks a time and can pay in full or a deposit on a payment page in your colours. The money goes straight to your Stripe account, and the amount already paid is deducted when you take the balance.

  • Deposit as a percentage or per participant
  • Requests gathered in the “Online” tab
  • No Lago fees on payments: only Stripe’s fees apply

Frequently asked questions

Escape rooms: your questions

Can players pay online?

+

Yes. You choose to ask for the full amount or a deposit, as a percentage or per participant. Payment happens on a page in your colours, with your own Stripe account: the money comes straight to you, and Lago never sees any card data.

Does Lago handle several game rooms?

+

Lago manages time slots, capacity, events with their room, and bookings from both the front desk and the website. If you run several rooms with different scenarios, show us how you work during the demo: we will tell you exactly what Lago covers today.

Can I sell gift cards?

+

Yes. Gift cards are created and redeemed directly at the till.
