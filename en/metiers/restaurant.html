Day to day

What changes with Lago

Your tables at a glance

Draw your room once: round, square or rectangular tables, placed by drag and drop. Each table shows its bill, covers and time elapsed, and turns blue when the kitchen is done.

  • One open bill per table, move a table in one tap
  • Takeaway sales at the counter, with the customer’s name
  • Live count of covers and open bills

One order per table, sent in one tap

The server takes the order on a tablet or at the till, adds a note (no onions, medium) and sends it: dishes go to the kitchen, drinks to the bar. Starters first, mains later: only what hasn’t been sent yet goes out.

  • Kitchen notes on every line
  • Send by course: starters, mains, desserts
  • Add dishes without resending what already left

Frequently asked questions

Restaurants: your questions

Does Lago handle the floor plan and sending orders to the kitchen?

+

Yes. You draw your room, each table carries its own bill, and orders go to the kitchen or the bar from the till. The kitchen follows them on a tablet screen, with a printable ticket if you prefer.

Can I send starters first, then mains?

+

Yes. When you send, you tick what goes now and what waits. What has already been sent is never sent again.

Do I need a kitchen printer?

+

No. A tablet is enough: tickets appear on screen with a sound alert. You can also print a ticket.

Can I take meal vouchers?

+

Yes, Lago records them as a separate payment method, visible on the ticket and in sales. The payment itself still goes through your usual terminal.

How do I manage allergens?

+

You fill in the 14 allergens for each product and print the table for your guests. Allergens also show on the order line. Checking your recipes remains your responsibility.

Does Lago help with food hygiene (HACCP)?

+

It offers daily checks (cold chain, cleaning), a monthly check and an incident report, validated with the manager’s name and exportable as a PDF. Your food-safety plan remains yours.

Can I have several VAT rates?

+

Yes. Each item has its own rate, and the ticket breaks VAT down by rate.

Can I split the bill?

+

Yes. A single ticket can be paid in several instalments and with several payment methods, for example partly by card and partly in cash.
