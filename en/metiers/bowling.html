Frequently asked questions

Bowling: your questions

Does Lago assign lanes?

+

Lago manages time slots, capacity and bookings. Automatic lane assignment is not available yet: let us know during the demo if it matters to you.

Can shoe hire go on the same ticket?

+

Yes. Shoe hire is an item like any other: it goes on the ticket with the games and the bar.

Are birthday parties handled?

+

Yes, as events with times, room and number of guests, shown in the schedule.
