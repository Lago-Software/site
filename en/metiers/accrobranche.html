What changes with Lago

Staggered departures, a calmer briefing

Every sale, at the counter or online, books a departure in the schedule. You see how many people are expected each quarter hour and spread the groups out.

  • Departures every 15 minutes
  • Groups and events on the same timeline
  • Online booking and payment

A waiver signed once for the year

The customer reads and signs the waiver on a tablet at reception. It stays valid for a year: on their next visit, the team checks in an instant that it has been signed, and the log can be downloaded as a PDF.

  • Customisable waiver text
  • Valid for one year

Frequently asked questions

Treetop adventure parks: your questions

Does Lago handle school groups and holiday clubs?

+

Yes, as events with times, number of participants and a quote. You can ask for an online deposit to confirm the visit.

Can I prepare different schedules for the summer?

+

Yes. The staff schedule is based on a standard week and special periods, such as school holidays.

Is the waiver valid for the whole season?

+

A waiver signed in Lago stays valid for one year. Have its text reviewed by your insurer.
