Frequently asked questions

Your questions

What equipment do I need?

+

A TV with a browser, or a tablet or computer plugged into the screen. You just open a web page: nothing to install on the TV.

How do I pair the TV?

+

In Lago, switch on the TV screen: a code appears. On the TV, open the screen page, type the code, then choose “Full screen”. A link and a QR code are also offered for a tablet.

Does the TV show data about my customers?

+

No. It only receives times, durations and numbers of people, plus the menu and prices from your catalogue. The content is signed by Lago: the TV ignores any modified content.

Do I need to keep Lago open?

+

Yes, on at least one device, such as the till: it updates the screen after every save and at least every 5 minutes. Your devices must also be linked online.

Does the voice announcement start by itself?

+

Browsers ask you to allow sound: just touch the TV screen, or press OK, once during set-up. The chime and announcement apply to the leisure screen.

Can I see the result before setting up the TV?

+

Yes. In Lago, “Preview” opens the screen with simulated times, with nothing plugged in.
