Create a works council in a minute: Lago generates its code, QR card and agreement. At the till the discount applies by itself, and every month you see what it represents.

At the till, the discount applies by itself

Active councils show up on the ticket in one tap. The employee gives their code or shows their card: type the code or scan the QR code and the discount is calculated.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions

Do council members need an account?

+

No. They show their QR card or give their code at the desk or the till: Lago does not create an account for them.

Can the discount be limited to some products or a period?

+

Yes. You choose the percentage, the product category concerned and the agreement dates.

Can several codes be combined on one ticket?

+

No, only one partner code applies per ticket.

Can the council book online with its code?

+

Not at the moment: the code is used at the desk or the till today.

Is the works council feature included in the plan?

+

Yes, it is included in the Complet plan, with no extra charge per council or per ticket.
